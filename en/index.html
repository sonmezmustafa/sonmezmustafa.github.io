About

I am a co-founder of Codigno Yazılım.

I am a METU Civil Engineering graduate.

I also worked on software projects while I was a student.

I try to abstract real life using the basic notions of engineering.

The effort to find the line between the professional and the amateur is, of course, in me too.

Contrary to the common view, I believe that doing different kinds of work across several fields is more effective than specialising in one.

It is a little harder, but it broadens your horizons.

Engineering today can be tossed about among technology, human relations, sales, foresight, the state of the country and habits.
